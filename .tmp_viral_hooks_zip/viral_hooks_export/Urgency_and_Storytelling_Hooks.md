# Urgency & Storytelling Hooks

**1.**
Hook: “You’re not ready for this part…”
Example: “When I finally checked my bank account after that deal… I wasn’t ready either.”
Psychology: Curiosity + tension. Teases payoff, forces viewer to stick.
**2.**
Hook: “This almost ruined me.”
Example: “One tiny mistake with my first client nearly destroyed my business.”
Psychology: Vulnerability + fear of loss. Makes people want the lesson.
**3.**
