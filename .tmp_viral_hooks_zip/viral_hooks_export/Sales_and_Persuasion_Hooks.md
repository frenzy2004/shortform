# Sales and Persuasion Hooks

**Hook 2301**
Hook: If you can’t explain it in 30 seconds, you can’t sell it.
Example: “Uber = tap a button, get a ride.”
Psychology: Clarity bias → short = memorable.
**Hook 2302**
Hook: People don’t buy when they understand you. They buy when they feel understood.
Example: A fitness coach sells more by saying “I know how it feels to be stuck,” than “my program has 50 workouts.”
Psychology: Empathy trigger.
**Hook 2303**
Hook: The biggest sales killer? Too many choices.
Example: One SaaS company cut 5 pricing tiers → sales jumped 37%.
Psychology: Choice paralysis.
**Hook 2304**

Example: Apple doesn’t sell cameras—it sells “memories in HD.”
Psychology: Future framing.
**Hook 2305**

Psychology: Urgency amplification.
**Hook 2306**
