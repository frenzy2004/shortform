# FOMO and Urgency Hooks

**3601**
Hook: This opportunity won’t be around tomorrow.
Example: Spots for my free workshop are closing tonight.
Psychology: Scarcity bias → people act faster when they believe availability is limited.
**3602**
Hook: Last chance to grab this before it’s gone.
Example: Enrollment closes at midnight—don’t miss it.
Psychology: Fear of missing out (FOMO) → urgency triggers action.
**3603**
Hook: 90% of people will see this and scroll… will you be the 10%?

Psychology: Exclusivity effect → being in the minority feels powerful.
**3604**
