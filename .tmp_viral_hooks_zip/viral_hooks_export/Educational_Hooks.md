# Educational Hooks

1. Hook: The most efficient way to ___ if you’re ___<br>Example: The most efficient way to lose fat if you’re slammed with work.<br>Psychology: Highlights efficiency under constraint; high self-relevance triggers attention.
2. Hook: How to get ___ fast<br>Example: How to get your site indexed on Google fast.<br>Psychology: Speed promise reduces perceived effort and increases urgency.
3. Hook: If you want ___, skip this and try this<br>Example: If you want focus, skip multitasking and try 50-minute sprints.<br>Psychology: Loss-avoidance + contrast frames the new tactic as safer.
4. Hook: Want to feel ___ without overspending?<br>Example: Want to feel confident in your wardrobe without overspending?<br>Psychology: Combines aspiration with cost relief (gain + pain relief).
5. Hook: Want ___? Then stop scrolling.<br>Example: Want more energy at work? Then stop scrolling—do this 5-minute reset.<br>Psychology: Pattern interrupt + direct call to action.
6. Hook: What the ___ industry won’t tell you about ___<br>Example: What the skincare industry won’t tell you about “miracle” serums.<br>Psychology: Curiosity via secrecy/insider knowledge.
7. Hook: Here’s why \[common belief\] is a myth<br>Example: Here’s why “eight glasses a day” is a myth.<br>Psychology: Belief-violation grabs attention and invites re-evaluation.
8. Hook: Here’s why \[common belief\] misleads most people<br>Example: Here’s why “work harder” misleads most professionals.<br>Psychology: Corrective insight signals authority and novelty.
9. Hook: Here’s why \[popular product\] is overrated<br>Example: Here’s why luxury face creams are overrated for anti-aging.<br>Psychology: Counter-trend stance sparks debate and clicks.
10. Hook: Here’s why \[popular advice\] isn’t working for you<br>Example: Here’s why “eat less, move more” isn’t working long-term.<br>Psychology: Empathy + promise of a targeted fix.
11. Hook: Here’s why you keep failing at \[task\]<br>Example: Here’s why you keep failing at weekly planning.<br>Psychology: Pattern recognition + coaching vibe builds trust.
